AREAPASS MARKETPLACE – PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 15, 2025

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Areapass. We (Peter Moss Ltd., referred to as "Areapass", "We", "Us", or "Our") are a company registered in Nigeria, committed to protecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your Personal Data when you use our website and services (collectively, the "Marketplace") from Nigeria. This policy is governed by the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

By using our Marketplace[a], you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. OUR ROLE AND THE PROMOTER'S ROLE

Areapass (Us / Peter Moss Ltd.): We are a Data Controller for the data you provide to create and maintain your Areapass account and use the Marketplace. We are responsible for the security and processing of this data to run the Marketplace.

The Event Promoter (e.g., Delphino): The Promoter is also a Data Controller for the data they need to manage their event (e.g., guest lists, event updates).

Joint Controllers: For the specific purpose of processing your ticket orders and facilitating your attendance at events, Areapass and the Promoter act as Joint Controllers. This means we share responsibility for ensuring your data is handled lawfully. We have an agreement in place to define our respective responsibilities—generally, Areapass handles the technology and security, while the Promoter handles event-specific communications.

3. WHAT PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

  • Account & Identity Data: Your name, email address, phone number, encrypted password.
  • Transaction Data: Details of tickets you purchase, order IDs, event names.
  • Payment Data: We do not store your full card information. This is handled directly by our payment processor (e.g., Paystack).
  • Technical Data: Your IP address, browser type, device type, location data, and cookies.
  • Support Data: Information you provide to our support team.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR PERSONAL DATA

We use your Personal Data based on a valid legal basis under the NDPA:

  • To Provide the Service: To create your account and process your ticket orders. (Basis: Performance of a Contract)
  • To Share with the Promoter: To allow the Promoter to manage the event and validate your ticket. (Basis: Performance of a Contract)
  • For Security & Fraud Prevention: To monitor for fraud and secure your account. (Basis: Legitimate Interest)
  • To Improve Our Service: To analyze how users interact with our site. (Basis: Legitimate Interest)
  • To Send You Marketing: (Basis: Consent)
  • To Comply with Legal Obligations: (Basis: Legal Obligation)

5. USE OF ANONYMIZED & AGGREGATED DATA

We reserve the right to anonymize and aggregate your Personal Data. We use this anonymized data (which is no longer Personal Data) for any lawful business purpose, such as training AI models, marketing analytics, and commercial use. Our basis for this is our Legitimate Interest.

6. WHO WE SHARE YOUR DATA WITH

We only share your Personal Data with the following parties:

A. The Promoter The specific Promoter of the event(s) you buy tickets for.

B. Payment Processors

  • Paystack (or similar): The payment gateway that securely processes your payment data.

C. Our Sub-processors (Technology Partners) We use global technology providers to host and maintain our platform. We have signed strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with them:

  • Grubchain Technologies Canada Inc.: Our core Data Processor that provides the Platform technology (Canada).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud infrastructure hosting (US/Canada).
  • Heroku (Salesforce): Application hosting (US).
  • Sentry: Error logging and performance monitoring (US).
  • Google Workspace: Internal productivity tools (Canada).

D. Legal & Law Enforcement If required by law or to protect our rights.

7. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

As a Nigerian company using global technology, your Personal Data will be transferred out of Nigeria. We ensure this is done lawfully under the NDPA 2023.

Transfer 1: Platform Data to Canada Our core platform partner (Grubchain) and internal tools (Google) are located in Canada.

  • Legal Basis: The NDPA recognizes Canada as providing an adequate level of data protection ("Adequacy"). We also use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for additional security.

Transfer 2: Infrastructure to the USA Our partners use US-based infrastructure (AWS, Heroku, Sentry).

  • Legal Basis: Since the US is not automatically recognized as "adequate," we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Data Processing Agreements to ensure your data remains protected to a Nigerian standard.

Transfer 3: Payment Data to Ireland/Global Our payment processor (Paystack) may transfer data to secure servers in Ireland or elsewhere.

  • Legal Basis: Transfers to Ireland are covered by EU Adequacy standards.

8. YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS (UNDER THE NDPA 2023)

You have the right to:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of your data.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limits on how we use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data be transferred.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing (e.g., for marketing).
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@areapass.xyz.

9. DATA SECURITY & RETENTION

  • Security: We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures (including encryption and access controls) to protect your data.
  • Retention: We retain your Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, or as required by law.
